Curriculum Vitae Professor Jon Rogers

I am professor of design at Northumbria University in Newcastle. I take a very hands-in approach to understanding human relationships with digital technologies by making things that help to uncover as yet untold stories of how our relationship with the internet and related technologies might unfold. Following a three year Senior Fellowship with Mozilla in Berlin (2016-2019) I led the Horizon 2020 Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral training programme OpenDoTT exploring trust and the internet of things. I have a long history of organising and delivering workshops that engage professionals, academics and the public with emerging technologies at Mozilla Festival, re:publica, Unbox Festival, London Design Festival, ThingsCon, ACM CHI and TEI.

Line management and mentoring

I have over 15 years of line managing and mentoring experience of early and mid-career researchers. I currently manage eight members of staff (including two research fellows, Dr Mike Shorter and Dr Dave Chatting) and mentor another eight. Previous ECR line management and support includes the five OpenDoTT ECRs, four Mozilla fellows and three research fellows. In addition I lead the Creative Technology Working group of ten people. I am committed to staff training and regularly run events and workshops to support learning in the school.

Creative Technology skills

I am skilled in both the Arduino Microcontroller and Raspberry Pi open hardware ecosystems using LoRa, TinyML and various networking standards (MQTT, ESP-NOW, UDP). Programming languages include C++, Java and Python. 3D printing (Prusa). CAD (Fusion 360, Shapr3D).
